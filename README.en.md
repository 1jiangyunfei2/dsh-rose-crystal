# Rose Crystal

**A DSH theme made for people who like soft pink**: blush pink-white gradients, jelly keycaps, a black cat-paw send button, and a composer with gently swaying cat ears.

---

## What it is

It replaces the default DeepSeek Harness look with a complete rose-crystal style. It is not just a wallpaper swap: colours, buttons and decorations all change together.

- **Palette**: blush pink-white gradients, dark text on light surfaces, comfortable for long sessions
- **Composer**: two cat ears on its top edge, joined seamlessly to the card, slowly swaying
- **Send button**: a black jelly cat-paw keycap with pink pads and toes
- **Buttons**: consistent pearl keycaps that bounce slightly on hover and drop small stars
- **Decorations**: four pink bows and eight small stars, breathing out of phase
- **Below the composer**: a small black cat and two pink hearts

## Who it is for

- People who like pink and want a softer interface
- People who want a little cute decoration without making the UI busy or hard to use
- People who spend long hours writing and want a comfortable-looking screen

## What you will see

| Area | Change |
|---|---|
| Background | Pink-white gradient with soft light layers |
| Composer | Pink-white gradient card with cat ears on top (animated) |
| Send button | Black cat-paw keycap |
| Regular buttons | Pearl keycaps, slight bounce on hover |
| Screen corners | Bows and small stars |

## Design rules

1. **Text always stays legible**: every label keeps dark, high-contrast colour
2. **Nothing is hidden**: only colours, radii, borders and decorations change; no button moves or loses its function
3. **Restrained motion**: all animation is subtle and respects the system "reduce motion" setting
4. **No data access**: a pure style plugin; it reads and reports nothing

## Compatibility

- Requires DeepSeek Harness 0.2.x
- Works with both light and dark themes
- Touches appearance only, never functional logic
